4 ROUTINES TO
BOOST YOUR
ENDURANCE.

JUL 28, 2026 · BY COACH RICARDO

Building endurance is about more than running longer or training harder. It's about developing consistency, improving your performance, and finding activities that keep you motivated.

Whether you're preparing for your next match or simply looking to improve your fitness, these four training approaches can help you develop your endurance.

01

INTERVAL TRAINING

Alternate periods of faster movement with easier recovery periods. This approach can help improve cardiovascular fitness while keeping your sessions varied and engaging.

02

STEADY-STATE CARDIO

Activities such as cycling, swimming, and jogging at a comfortable, consistent pace help develop aerobic endurance over time.

03

SPORT-SPECIFIC TRAINING

Practice movements related to your favorite sport. Football, padel, and basketball all challenge your body in different ways and help build useful athletic skills.

04

RECOVERY AND CONSISTENCY

Progress also depends on rest, hydration, sleep, and allowing your body to recover. Consistent, balanced training is more valuable than pushing yourself too hard.
